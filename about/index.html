What's in the game

Everything below is in the game today.

UK driving, in mph

Traffic keeps left, speeds and limits are in mph, and UK speed limits and speed cameras apply. Break the rules and you'll pick up fines.

Islands, cities and motorways

Three landmasses split by winding sea channels, with the cities of Albion, Sandvale and Kingsbridge plus smaller towns. The M1 orbital runs round the coast, and the M2 and M3 cut through the middle over long bridges.

Deliveries and the job board

Pick local or long-haul jobs from the job board, couple the trailer, drive it to the bay and unload. Earn cash and XP, climb the tiers, buy trucks at the dealership, then buy your own haulage yards and homes.

Six trailer types

Curtain-sider, fridge box, container skeletal, flatbed, tanker and an army container. Each job shows the trailer and its weight before you take it.

Fuel, damage and garages

Watch the fuel gauge and fill up at the pumps. Knocks add damage, and at 100% you break down. Drive into a repair bay at a garage to fix the truck, or visit the tune shop next door.

LGV training ground

The Kingsbridge LGV Training Ground has a timed cone loop: cross the start line, pass both checkpoints and finish without touching a cone. Your best time is saved. There's also an emergency-stop light, a wet skid pan and practice docks.

Lift bridges and ships

Two lift bridges cross the shipping channels. The leaves lift and traffic waits while ships sail through to the living container ports at Albion and Kingsbridge, where cranes work the quays.

UK junctions and lights

Signal-controlled junctions with UK light phasing, yellow box junctions, lane arrows, roundabouts and flyovers. Indicate with Q and E; they cancel themselves.

Pedestrians and traffic

People walk along pavements, through parks and over crossings. AI cars, lorries and motorbikes fill the roads, and AI hauliers work the ports.

Day, night and weather

A full day and night cycle with street lights, lit windows and headlights (L for full beam). The weather rolls through clear spells, rain, fog and storms, and wet roads cut your grip.

Emergency services

Police patrol and give chase if you're wanted. Fires break out and the nearest fire station sends an engine. Paramedics come out to treat casualties, and helicopters fly from the military base.

Invite-code multiplayer

Host a session and share its invite code or link. Friends join straight into your world from their browser, with nothing to install.

Sat nav and world map

Open the map with M, pick a destination and the sat nav plans a route along the roads, with guidance arrows in the world. The minimap is always in the corner.

FAQ

Quick answers.

Is it free?

Yes. It runs in your web browser at trucksim.co.uk, with nothing to download or buy.

Does it work on phones?

Yes. Phones get touch controls: a landscape layout with pedals and steering in the side gutters, or a portrait handheld deck. Graphics drop to Low automatically if the frame rate struggles, and you can also choose High or Low yourself in Settings.

Can I play with friends?

Yes. Host a game and share its invite code or link. Friends join your world straight from their browser, connected peer to peer.

Can I use a controller?

Yes. Any Xbox-style controller works through the browser alongside the keyboard, and the on-screen prompts switch to controller buttons.

Which side of the road do you drive on?

The left, as in the UK. Speeds and limits are in mph, and the junctions, lights and road markings follow UK conventions.

Is my progress saved?

Yes. Your career, cash, trucks and best training-ground time are saved in your browser.

Ready to roll?

Start the engine. The first job is waiting on the board.
